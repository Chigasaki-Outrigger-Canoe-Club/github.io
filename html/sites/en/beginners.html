FOR BEGINNERS

Chigasaki Outrigger Canoe Club welcomes new members at any time!
Whatever your age, gender or sports background, if you want to get moving on the sea and make new friends, come and try a session first!
Feel how wonderful the ocean is, and we hope you'll join us 🌊

How a trial session works Trial at a glance About the club FAQ

How a trial session works

  1. Contact usGet in touch through the contact page or by Instagram DM.
  2. Apply for a trial sessionWe will ask which dates suit you and choose a trial day based on sea conditions.
  3. Trial dayFrom meeting up to wrapping up takes about 3 to 4 hours.
  4. Joining the clubIf you would like to keep paddling, we will explain how to join. We plan to let you complete the process through our official LINE account in the future.

Experienced members give you a lesson on land, like this.

Sample schedule for the day
TimeWhat we do
6:00Meet up, check in, change
6:30On land: how to hold the paddle, getting in and out, safety briefing
7:00Out on the water for your trial
9:00Back on the beach and clean up
9:30Wrap up

* We can adjust the times to suit you.
* Times may change, or the session may be canceled, depending on the weather and sea conditions.

What to bring
  • Clothes that can get wet (swimsuit, rash guard, etc.)
  • Towel and a change of clothes
  • Something to drink
  • Hat and sunscreen
  • Sandals or marine shoes with a heel strap
