ABOUT COCC

About COCC

Chigasaki Outrigger Canoe Club (COCC) is a sports club based on the sea at Chigasaki, bringing together paddlers of all generations.

  • Safety comes first. We check sea conditions and keep our activities within safe limits. [TBC]
  • From beginners to experienced paddlers, everyone can enjoy it according to their own goals. [TBC]

OUTRIGGER CANOE

What is an outrigger canoe?

It is a canoe from Polynesia with a float, called an "ama," extending out from the side of the hull. The float keeps the canoe stable, and in the six-person canoe (OC6), where six people paddle in time together, members of different ages and fitness levels can share the same canoe and enjoy it.
