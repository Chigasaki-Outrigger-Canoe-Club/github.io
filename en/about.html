PROFILE

About Masamitsu Shiozawa

The profile below is based on an interview published in 2018 and is awaiting his confirmation. [TBC]

NameMasamitsu Shiozawa
RolesRepresentative Director of NPO Pacific Beach Club; diving instructor; outrigger canoe paddler
BackgroundAfter university he joined a trading company, then left and moved to Australia, where he worked as a bus driver, tour guide and diving instructor. Back in Japan he ran a diving shop and founded a snowboard school.
Outrigger canoeHe discovered outrigger canoeing in Hawaii, brought a canoe to Japan and started Chigasaki Outrigger Canoe Club.
Community workThrough NPO Pacific Beach Club he runs programs that let children experience the ocean and its environment, including a kids' camp in Ogasawara.
