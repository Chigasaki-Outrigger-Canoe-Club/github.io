General inquiries

We welcome questions about trial sessions, visits and joining at any time. Including the following in your message helps us reply smoothly.

  • Your name
  • What you are interested in (trial session, visit, joining, etc.)
  • Your preferred date
  • Whether you have any experience

For now, we accept inquiries by Instagram DM. A contact form and an official LINE account are still being prepared. [TBC]

Send a DM on Instagram

For trial sessions for children, please see the COCC KIDS page.

Press and media inquiries

We accept media requests from TV, newspapers, magazines, web media and others. Please let us know the following. [TBC]

  • Name of the outlet and the name of the person in charge
  • What the coverage is about and its purpose
  • Your preferred date
  • Planned publication or broadcast date

We may ask you to change the date depending on ocean conditions.

Corporate sponsorship

We are looking for companies and organizations who would like to support COCC's activities and the development of our young paddlers. We decide the details of sponsorship together after talking with you. [TBC]

  • Support for activities: Help us maintain canoes and equipment and travel to races. [TBC]
  • Support for KIDS: Help us support activities that let children get to know the sea. [TBC]
  • Help at events: We welcome your help at trial sessions and community events. [TBC]

Anything else

If your question doesn't fit any of the above, feel free to get in touch. We will review your message and a staff member will reply. It may take a few days to get back to you. [TBC]
