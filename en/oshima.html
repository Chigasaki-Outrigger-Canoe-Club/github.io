THE CROSSING

Paddling to the island

On a clear day, stand on the beach at Chigasaki and you can see an island floating beyond the horizon. Izu Oshima. We cross to that island under our own power. The Oshima Crossing is a once-a-year voyage that Chigasaki Outrigger Canoe Club started.

Out on the open sea, your phone has no signal. All you can rely on is the crew in your canoe and the paddle in your hands. You call to each other, match your breathing and simply keep paddling. Out there you meet the sea, and each other, in a way everyday life never shows you.

Then the island draws near and the canoe slides up onto the beach. A sense of achievement beyond words, and a bond with your crew, fills your chest. It is a moment only those who paddled together can share.

  • Aim for a distant island

    A shape on the horizon slowly becomes land. You feel every stroke of the distance.

  • Trust your crew

    No phones, no shortcuts. Six paddles moving as one is what carries the canoe forward.

  • Beyond the last stroke

    The achievement and the unity at the finish stay with you long after the day is over.
