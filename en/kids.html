About the KIDS program

COCC KIDS is a club that nurtures children who truly enjoy the sea off Chigasaki. Centered on outrigger canoeing, kids learn both the fun and the challenges of the ocean, and build the sense of accomplishment that comes from seeing things through with teammates.

Practice photos and Instagram

We share our everyday practice on the COCC KIDS Instagram. You can also request a trial session by DM.
