TRIAL SESSIONS

Trial sessions

How do I sign up for a trial session?

Contact us through the contact page or by Instagram DM. We will ask for your preferred dates and set a day based on sea conditions. See how a trial session works. [TBC]

Can I join if I can't swim?

Everyone wears a life jacket. If you are not confident in the water, let us know when you sign up. [TBC]

I'm not very fit. Is that a problem?

Six people paddle together, so you can go at your own pace. People of any age and experience can enjoy it.

Do I need my own gear?

The club provides paddles and life jackets. [TBC]

What should I bring?

Clothes that can get wet (swimwear, a rash guard), a towel, a change of clothes, a drink, a hat and sunscreen. Sandals with a heel strap or water shoes work well. [TBC]

Can I come on my own?

Of course. Many people come by themselves.

What happens if it rains or is windy?

If sea conditions are poor, we cancel or postpone for safety. We will contact you by the day before. [TBC]

CHILDREN

Children

Can children try it?

Elementary to high school students can join the COCC KIDS program.

How do you keep children safe?

Coaches watch from both the shore and the water, and every child wears a life jacket. See Safety for details. [TBC]

RACES

Races

Can I join without racing?

Yes. From beginners to experienced paddlers, everyone can enjoy the club at their own level. [TBC]

Does COCC host any races?

We host two: the COCC Hukilau Challenge and the All Japan Women's Ho'aikane.
