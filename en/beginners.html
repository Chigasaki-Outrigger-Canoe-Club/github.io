OUTRIGGER CANOE

What is an outrigger canoe?

It is a canoe from Polynesia with a float, called an "ama," extending out from the side of the hull. The float keeps the canoe stable, and in the six-person canoe (OC6), where six people paddle in time together, members of different ages and fitness levels can share the same canoe and enjoy it.

ABOUT COCC

About COCC

Chigasaki Outrigger Canoe Club (COCC) is a sports club based on the sea at Chigasaki, bringing together paddlers of all generations.

  • Safety comes first. We check sea conditions and keep our activities within safe limits. [TBC]
  • From beginners to experienced paddlers, everyone can enjoy it according to their own goals. [TBC]
  • We care for the sea and beach of Chigasaki and create opportunities for children to get to know the ocean. [TBC]

FAQ

Common questions

Can I take part if I can't swim?

You will wear a life jacket. If you are worried about swimming, please let us know when you apply. [TBC]

I'm not confident in my fitness.

Because six people paddle together, you can join at your own pace. It is a sport anyone can enjoy, regardless of age or experience.

Do I need my own equipment?

The club provides paddles and life jackets. [TBC]

What happens on rainy or windy days?

If sea conditions are poor, we cancel or postpone for safety. We will contact you by the day before. [TBC]

Can I join on my own?

Of course. Many people come by themselves.

Can children try it too?

For elementary through high school students, we offer the COCC KIDS program.
