SAFETY

Sea rules, emergencies and site rules

The sea looks different every day. COCC puts safety above everything else. The following is what we plan to confirm and finalize as a club. [TBC]

Rules of the sea

  • Before launching, everyone checks the wind, waves, tide and weather forecast together.
  • Do not push yourself if you feel unwell or are short on sleep.
  • On the water, follow the steersperson and the leader.
  • If in doubt, don't go out. Don't hesitate to turn back.
  • Never go out alone. Always go out in a group.

Emergency response [TBC]

  1. First, check that everyone is safeCount heads and make sure no one is hurt.
  2. Stay with the canoeIf the canoe capsizes, hold on to it and right it following the proper procedure.
  3. Contact shoreLaunch with a way to communicate, and contact the shore person in charge when needed.
  4. For accidents at sea, call 118For accidents on the water, call the Japan Coast Guard (dial 118). For injuries or sudden illness on land, call an ambulance or the fire department (dial 119).

Site rules [TBC]

  • On the beach, give priority to other users such as surfers and swimmers, and be considerate.
  • Keep canoes and equipment in the designated areas and do not block walkways.
  • Always take your trash home. Rinse used equipment with fresh water and put it away.
  • Park cars and bicycles in the designated places.
  • Prepare quietly in the early morning so as not to disturb the neighbors.

  • Summer precautions

    Watch out for heatstroke and sunburn. Drink water often, and use sunscreen and a hat. When jellyfish are around, cover up more of your skin.

  • Winter precautions

    Watch out for hypothermia. Staying wet can chill you quickly. Bring a windproof jacket and be ready to change as soon as you come ashore.
