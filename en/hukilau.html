ABOUT

About the event

The Hukilau Challenge is a six-person outrigger canoe (OC6) race hosted by Chigasaki Outrigger Canoe Club. As an event sanctioned by the Japan Outrigger Canoe Association (JOCA), it brings clubs from all over Japan to the waters off Chigasaki.

The information on this page is based on the previous edition (the 18th, held November 2–3, 2024). We will update it as soon as the dates and race rules for the next edition are decided. [TBC]

Fees and entry (previous edition)

ItemFee
Each class30,000 yen per team

  • If you are entering a Masters class, please submit proof of age (a copy of your driver's license or passport).
  • Double entries are not allowed (except for the Club Relay).
  • We recommend that each participant take out personal accident insurance.
  • If the event is canceled due to severe weather, we will refund 12,000 yen per team.
